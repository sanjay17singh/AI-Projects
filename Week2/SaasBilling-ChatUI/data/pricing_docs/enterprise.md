# Enterprise Plan

**Custom pricing** — contact sales.

Includes everything in Business, plus:
- Unlimited team seats
- Custom API call volume with dedicated rate limits
- Custom storage allocation
- Dedicated customer success manager
- 99.9% uptime SLA with credits for downtime
- Custom contract terms, invoicing, and payment terms (wire, ACH, NET-30/NET-60)

Enterprise plans are not self-serve. Existing Business customers who need Enterprise-level scale should be routed to the sales team rather than self-upgrading.
