# Annual vs Monthly Billing

All plans (Starter, Pro, Business) can be billed monthly or annually:

- **Monthly**: full price charged every 30 days, maximum flexibility to change or cancel.
- **Annual**: paid once a year, equivalent to roughly 2 months free compared to 12 months of monthly billing. Best for teams confident in their plan tier for the year ahead.

You can switch billing frequency at any time from Settings > Billing > Subscription. Switching from monthly to annual applies any unused monthly credit to the first annual invoice. Switching from annual to monthly mid-term converts remaining unused annual value into an account credit applied to future monthly invoices.
